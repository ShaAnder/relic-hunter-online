# Items, Inventory & Win Condition

---

## Categories

| Category | Role |
|----------|------|
| Relic | The one win-condition item for the match |
| Loot | General pickups, compendium-worthy |
| Gear | Weapon / Armor / Accessory slots — empty placeholders for now, no stats |

---

## Inventory

9 slots total:

- 3 fixed Gear slots (empty for now)
- 6 general slots (Relic + Loot)

Pickup is automatic on walk. If the 6 general slots are full the item stays on the ground. No swap prompt.

---

## Win condition

Stand on the Exit tile while holding the match's Relic, arrived there with a normal Move.

The Blue **E** card does **not** trigger the win. It only teleports you to the exit then bounces you to a random tile. That is intentional, not unfinished.

---

## Compendium

An item unlocks only if it is still in your inventory when you win the match. Dropped or left-behind items do not count. Relic and Loot only for now.

---

## Match result screen

Win/loss, turns taken, items extracted, return to Lobby. Deeper stats later.
